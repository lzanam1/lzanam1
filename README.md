### 🔬 Projects

#### [Deep Hedging under Transaction Costs](https://github.com/lzanam1/neural_option_hedging) · Python, PyTorch
A recurrent neural network that hedges a short option position under 1% transaction costs,
trained end-to-end through a Monte Carlo simulator to minimise **CVaR95**.
- Out-of-sample: **−41% tail risk** vs Black–Scholes delta hedging and **−8%** vs the best tuned
  classical no-trade band, robust across 5 training seeds
- Every building block validated against theory (Black–Scholes pricing, 1/√N hedging error,
  recovery of the BS delta without costs)
-  [Technical report (PDF)](https://github.com/lzanam1/neural_option_hedging/blob/main/docs/report.pdf)

#### [Bankruptcy Prediction](https://github.com/lzanam1/bankruptcy-prediction) · Python, XGBoost
Predicting bankruptcy of Polish companies from financial ratios.
- Missing data handled with **MICE**, with a comparison of imputation strategies
- **Optuna** tuning optimised for the **F2 score** to handle class imbalance
- **SHAP** interpretation of the model and an honest limitations section
